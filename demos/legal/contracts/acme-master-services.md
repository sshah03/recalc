# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Acme Logistics, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 400 Harbor Way, Wilmington, DE 19801 ("Customer"), and Northwind Analytics LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("Provider").

## 1. Services

1.1 Provider shall perform the services described in one or more statements of work executed by the parties (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

1.2 Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive one (1) year periods (each, a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Customer shall pay the fees set forth in each SOW. Undisputed invoices are due within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

3.2 Late payments accrue interest at the lesser of 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

## 4. Termination

4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.

4.2 Customer may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice, provided that Customer pays all fees for Services performed through the effective date of termination.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall hold the other party's Confidential Information in confidence for a period of five (5) years following disclosure, except that trade secrets shall be protected for so long as they remain trade secrets.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

6.1 EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, BREACH OF SECTION 5, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

6.2 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES.

## 7. Assignment

7.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld; provided, however, that either party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets.

## 8. Governing Law and Disputes

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

8.2 The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

## 9. General

9.1 This Agreement, together with all SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements relating to its subject matter.

9.2 Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
