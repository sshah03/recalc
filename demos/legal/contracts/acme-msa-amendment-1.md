# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "Amendment") is entered into as of November 20, 2025 by and between Acme Logistics, Inc. ("Customer") and Northwind Analytics LLC ("Provider"), and amends the Master Services Agreement between the parties dated March 3, 2025 (the "Agreement"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Limitation of Liability

Section 6.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

"6.1 EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, BREACH OF SECTION 5, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL EXCEED TWO TIMES (2X) THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM."

## 2. Change of Control

A new Section 7.2 is added to the Agreement:

"7.2 Notwithstanding Section 7.1, a Change of Control of Provider shall be deemed an assignment requiring Customer's prior written consent. 'Change of Control' means any transaction or series of transactions in which a person or group acquires beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of Provider's voting equity, or all or substantially all of Provider's assets. Provider shall give Customer written notice at least thirty (30) days before the closing of any Change of Control. If Customer does not consent, Customer may terminate the Agreement on written notice without liability for early termination."

## 3. Fees

The hourly rates in SOW #2 are increased by four percent (4%) effective January 1, 2026.

## 4. Effect

Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect. In the event of a conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment controls.
