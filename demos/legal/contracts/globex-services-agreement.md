# SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of July 15, 2024 between Globex Corporation, a New York corporation ("Globex"), and Northwind Analytics LLC ("Northwind").

## 1. Engagement

Northwind will provide data analytics and contract staffing services to Globex as described in Exhibit A.

## 2. Term and Renewal

The term of this Agreement is one (1) year from the date above. The Agreement will renew automatically for additional one-year terms unless either party delivers written notice of termination at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the current term.

## 3. Compensation

Globex will pay Northwind a placement fee equal to twenty percent (20%) of the first-year base salary for each direct hire, and the hourly rates in Exhibit A for contract personnel. Invoices are payable net thirty (30).

## 4. Termination

4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days of notice.

4.2 Globex may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Northwind.

## 5. Non-Solicitation

During the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Globex shall not directly solicit for employment any Northwind employee who provided services under this Agreement.

## 6. Intellectual Property

All work product created by contract personnel in the course of providing services to Globex shall be the sole property of Globex.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Northwind's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Globex to Northwind during the six (6) months immediately preceding the claim.

7.2 The limitation in Section 7.1 does not apply to Northwind's breach of Section 6 or to claims arising from Northwind's infringement of third-party intellectual property rights, for which Northwind's liability is unlimited.

## 8. Assignment

Northwind may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without Globex's prior written consent. Globex may assign this Agreement to any affiliate or to a successor of its business upon written notice to Northwind.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in New York County administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules.

## 10. Insurance

Northwind shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.
