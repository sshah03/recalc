# OFFICE LEASE

This Lease is made as of June 1, 2023 between Harborview Properties LP, a Massachusetts limited partnership ("Landlord"), and Northwind Analytics LLC ("Tenant").

## 1. Premises

Landlord leases to Tenant approximately 6,200 rentable square feet on the fourth floor of 55 Seaport Boulevard, Boston, Massachusetts (the "Premises").

## 2. Term

2.1 The term of this Lease is five (5) years, commencing June 1, 2023 and expiring May 31, 2028 (the "Term").

2.2 Tenant has one option to extend the Term for an additional three (3) years at the then-prevailing market rent, exercisable by written notice given not less than nine (9) months and not more than twelve (12) months before the expiration of the Term. This Lease does not renew automatically.

## 3. Rent

3.1 Base Rent is $31,000 per month for the first Lease Year, increasing three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the commencement date.

3.2 Tenant shall pay its proportionate share (11.4%) of Operating Expenses and Taxes as Additional Rent.

## 4. Security Deposit

Tenant has deposited $93,000 as security for its obligations under this Lease.

## 5. Use

The Premises shall be used for general office purposes and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

## 6. Assignment and Subletting

6.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold in its reasonable discretion.

6.2 For purposes of this Section 6, any transfer of a controlling interest in Tenant, whether by merger, consolidation, sale of equity, or otherwise, shall be deemed an assignment of this Lease.

6.3 Notwithstanding the foregoing, Landlord's consent shall not be required for an assignment to an entity that acquires all or substantially all of Tenant's assets and has a tangible net worth, immediately following the transaction, of not less than $25,000,000, provided Tenant gives Landlord thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

## 7. Termination

Tenant has no right to terminate this Lease early. Landlord may terminate this Lease upon an Event of Default that remains uncured after the applicable notice and cure period set out in Section 14.

## 8. Insurance and Indemnity

8.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $3,000,000 per occurrence naming Landlord as an additional insured.

8.2 Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against claims arising from Tenant's use of the Premises, except to the extent caused by Landlord's negligence or willful misconduct.

## 9. Limitation of Landlord's Liability

Landlord's liability under this Lease shall be limited to Landlord's interest in the Building, and Tenant shall look solely to such interest for the satisfaction of any judgment against Landlord.

## 10. Governing Law

This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
