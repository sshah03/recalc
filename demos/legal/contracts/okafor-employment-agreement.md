# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Executive Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of February 1, 2024 between Northwind Analytics LLC (the "Company") and Priya Okafor (the "Executive").

## 1. Position and Duties

The Company employs the Executive as Chief Technology Officer, reporting to the Managing Member. The Executive shall devote substantially all business time to the Company.

## 2. Term

The Executive's employment is at will and may be terminated by either party at any time, subject to the notice and severance provisions of Section 6. This Agreement has no fixed term.

## 3. Compensation

3.1 Base salary of $310,000 per year, reviewed annually.

3.2 Annual target bonus of forty percent (40%) of base salary, subject to achievement of goals set by the Managing Member.

3.3 A grant of profits interests representing 2.5% of the Company's fully diluted equity, vesting over four (4) years with a one-year cliff.

## 4. Change of Control

4.1 If, within twelve (12) months following a Change of Control, the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, then all unvested profits interests shall vest in full and the Executive shall receive the severance described in Section 6.2 at two (2) times the amounts stated there.

4.2 "Change of Control" means the sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets, or a transaction in which the holders of the Company's voting equity immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the surviving entity immediately after it.

## 5. Restrictive Covenants

5.1 Non-competition. During employment and for twelve (12) months after termination for any reason, the Executive shall not, within the United States, be employed by or provide services to any business that develops or sells supply-chain analytics software competitive with the Company's products.

5.2 Non-solicitation. During employment and for eighteen (18) months after termination, the Executive shall not solicit any employee or customer of the Company.

5.3 The Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 5 are reasonable and supported by the consideration provided under this Agreement, including the equity grant in Section 3.3.

## 6. Termination and Severance

6.1 Either party may terminate employment on thirty (30) days' written notice; the Company may pay salary in lieu of notice.

6.2 On termination by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason, the Executive shall receive six (6) months of base salary continuation and COBRA premium reimbursement for the same period, conditioned on a signed release.

## 7. Confidentiality and Inventions

The Executive shall keep the Company's confidential information confidential during and after employment and assigns to the Company all inventions conceived in the course of employment.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado. Any dispute shall be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Denver, Colorado.
