# Tamsin Labs, Inc. — Human Resources Security Standard

| | |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.1 |
| Effective date | 2025-09-01 |
| Owner | VP People (Dana Okonkwo-Reyes) |

## 1. Scope

This standard applies to every employee and contractor of Tamsin Labs, Inc., in every role and location.

## 2. Pre-employment screening

2.1 Before a candidate's start date, and before any account is provisioned, People Operations completes screening through a third-party screening provider. Screening covers identity verification, criminal record history where permitted by local law, and verification of the two most recent employers.

2.2 No workforce member is given access to production systems or customer data until screening has been completed and the result recorded in the HRIS.

2.3 Contractors with access to customer data are screened to the same standard, either by Tamsin or by their employer under contract.

## 3. Terms of employment

Every workforce member signs a confidentiality agreement and acknowledges the Information Security Policy before their first day.

## 4. Offboarding

On termination, the manager and People Operations raise an offboarding ticket the same day. IT removes access within one business day and recovers company devices.
