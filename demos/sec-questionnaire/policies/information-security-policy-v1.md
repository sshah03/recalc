## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the minimum security requirements for Tamsin Labs, Inc. and applies to all employees and contractors.

## 2. Encryption

Customer data is encrypted at rest in production databases and in transit using TLS.

## 3. Access control

3.1 Access is granted on the principle of least privilege.

3.2 Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access to the corporate network and for administrator accounts.

3.3 User access is reviewed semi-annually.

## 4. Change management

Production changes are made through pull requests and reviewed where practical.

## 5. Business continuity and disaster recovery

5.1 The recovery time objective (RTO) for the production service is **24 hours**. The recovery point objective (RPO) is 24 hours.

5.2 Production databases are backed up daily.

## 6. Incident response

6.1 Tamsin maintains an incident response procedure.

6.2 Customers affected by a confirmed security incident are notified within 72 hours of confirmation.

## 7. Security awareness

New starters complete security awareness training during onboarding.
