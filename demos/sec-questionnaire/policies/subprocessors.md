# Tamsin Labs sub-processors

Last updated 2026-06-12. These third parties store or process customer data on Tamsin's behalf.

| Sub-processor | Service | Customer data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services | Cloud infrastructure (compute, databases, object storage) | All customer data |
| Postwick Mail, Inc. | Transactional email | Names and email addresses of customer users |
| Quillmoth AI, Inc. | Large language model API used by the "Explain this chart" feature | Chart metadata and user-entered question text; no retention by the vendor under contract |
| Ferrous Support Systems Ltd. | Customer support ticketing | Content customers include in support tickets |

Vendors that never receive customer data (for example, payroll and office software) are not sub-processors and are not listed.
