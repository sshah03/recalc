# Tamsin Labs, Inc. — Vendor Management Policy

| | |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.4 |
| Effective date | 2025-11-01 |
| Owner | Head of Security |

## 1. Scope

Any third party that stores, processes or has access to customer data ("sub-processor"), or that provides a service the platform depends on.

## 2. Onboarding

Before a sub-processor receives customer data, Security reviews its most recent independent assurance report (SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certificate), and Legal executes a data processing agreement with confidentiality, security and breach-notification terms.

## 3. Annual review

Sub-processors are reviewed at least annually. The review covers the vendor's latest assurance report, open findings, and any change in the data it processes.

## 4. Customer notice

Customers are notified at least 30 days before a new sub-processor begins processing their data. The current list is maintained in `subprocessors.md`.
