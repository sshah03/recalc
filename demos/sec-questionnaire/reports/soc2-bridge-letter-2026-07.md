# Bridge letter

Tamsin Labs, Inc.
8 July 2026

To our customers and prospective customers,

Tamsin Labs' most recent SOC 2 Type II report, issued by Pellham & Voss LLP, covers the period 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025. Our next SOC 2 Type II report will cover 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026 and is expected in February 2027.

For the period from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026 (the "bridge period"), management confirms that, to the best of its knowledge:

1. There have been no material changes to the design of the controls described in the 2025 report, other than adoption of Information Security Policy v2.0 on 1 March 2026.
2. We are not aware of any security incident that affected the confidentiality, integrity or availability of customer data.
3. We are not aware of any control failure that would have a material effect on the opinion expressed in the 2025 report.

This letter is provided by management and has not been audited.

Rowan Achterberg, Chief Technology Officer
