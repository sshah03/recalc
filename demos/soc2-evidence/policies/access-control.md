# Brindlewood, Inc. — Access Control Policy (v3.1, effective 2025-10-01)

## 1. Authentication

1.1 Every active identity provider (IdP) account must be enrolled in multi-factor authentication. Deprovisioned accounts are out of scope.

## 2. Deprovisioning

2.1 IT removes a terminated workforce member's IdP access within **one business day** of the termination date recorded in the HRIS. Business days are Monday to Friday.

2.2 Access removed on or before the termination date (for example, at the start of garden leave) meets this requirement.

## 3. Access reviews

3.1 System owners review user access to production systems each calendar quarter.

3.2 The review for a quarter is completed and signed by the Head of Security **within 30 days after quarter end**. A review signed later is a control exception, even if the review itself was thorough.
