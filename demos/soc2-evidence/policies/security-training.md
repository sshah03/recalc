# Brindlewood, Inc. — Security Awareness Training Standard (v1.2, effective 2025-07-01)

1. Every new workforce member completes the "Security awareness: new starter" course **no later than 30 calendar days after their hire date**. The hire date is day 0, so a completion on day 30 is on time and a completion on day 31 is late.
2. All workforce members complete the annual security awareness course each calendar year.
3. Completions are recorded in the learning management system and exported for audit.
