# Brindlewood, Inc. — Vendor Management Policy (v1.3, effective 2025-10-01)

1. Security keeps an inventory of vendors and marks as critical any vendor that stores payroll or bank data or that production depends on.
2. Each critical vendor is reviewed at least annually. The review covers the vendor's latest SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate, any exceptions in it, and the complementary user-entity controls we must operate.
3. Each review is recorded on the vendor review form, signed by the Head of Security, and kept with the audit evidence.
